Boundaries
Use StaticBody2D nodes to define the game's borders, preventing 
the ball from going out of bounds.
Paddles
Create KinematicBody2D nodes for the paddles, controlled by 
players. They will bounce the ball.
Ball
Use a KinematicBody2D for the ball, which bounces off the paddles 
and boundaries. The ball is the core element of the game.
