Sprite Sheets
Sprite sheets efficiently organize multiple sprites 
within a single image, saving memory and 
improving performance.
What are Sprites?
Sprites are the individual images used to create 
the visuals of your game. They represent 
characters, objects, and background elements.
Types of Sprites
Common sprite types include character sprites, 
object sprites, and tile-based sprites for 
backgrounds.

• FRAME-BY-FRAME ANIMATION
This technique involves creating multiple individual frames of 
animation that are displayed in sequence to create movement.
• SPRITE SHEETS
Sprite sheets can be used to efficiently store and manage 
the multiple frames needed for animation.
• ANIMATION TIMING
Adjusting the speed at which frames are displayed controls 
the pace and fluidity of the animation.
