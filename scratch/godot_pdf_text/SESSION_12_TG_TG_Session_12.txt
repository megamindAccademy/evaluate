 ●  A  Collision  Shape  must  be  added  to  define  its

Quiz  Answers  
1.  How  can  we  play  sounds  in  Godot?  
●  Use  AudioStreamPlayer  or  
AudioStreamPlayer2D
.
 ●  Load  an  audio  file  and  use  play() to  play  it.  
2.  How  can  sound  enhance  the  player  experience?  
●  Add  sound  effects  when  a  point  is  scored.  ●  Play  background  music  during  gameplay.  ●  Include  sound  effects  when  the  ball  hits  the  
paddle.
 
3.  How  do  you  stop  all  sounds  in  Godot?  
●  Use  stop() on  all  AudioStreamPlayers .  ●  Another  way  is  setting  the  volume  to  zero  using  AudioServer.set_bus_volume_db().
