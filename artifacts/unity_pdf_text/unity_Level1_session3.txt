--- Page 6 ---
All rights reserved
Script Machine
Embed: This is a graph that is contained directly inside the GameObject: the asset is saved to the 
GameObject, and not to a drive.
Note: Every modification you make in the Inspector during Play mode (runtime) is reset (lost) when 
you exit Play mode. For the edit to persist, you must make it outside Play mode.
Graph: This is a normal graph. A Macro generates a new file that saves the asset on a drive and 
not to the GameObject.
Note: Use graphs to get scripts that are shareable and that you can edit in the Inspector during 
Play mode (runtime) without losing your modifications. Graphs are ideal for prototyping.
