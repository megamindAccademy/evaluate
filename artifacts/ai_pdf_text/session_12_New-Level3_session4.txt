Player Win
The logic used to determine if the player wins is as follows:
•If the player chooses Rock (represented by 1) and the AI chooses Scissors (represented by 3)
•If the player chooses Paper (represented by 2) and the AI chooses Rock (represented by 1)
•If the player chooses Scissors (represented by 3) and the AI chooses Paper (represented by 2)
In each of these cases, the player wins and their score is incremented.

AI Win
The conditions in the if statement check if the playerMove is 3 (rock) and the randomNumber is 1 (paper), or if the playerMove 
is 1 (paper) and the randomNumber is 2 (scissors), or if the playerMove is 2 (scissors) and the randomNumber is 3 (rock).
If any of these conditions are true, it means that the AI has won the round, and the score of the AI (which is stored in the first 
element of the scores list) is incremented by 1.
