What is Chat GPT?
Chatgpt is an artificial intelligence 
chatbot developed by OpenAI and 
launched in November 2022. It is built on 
top of OpenAI's GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 
families of large language models and 
has been fine-tuned using both 
supervised and reinforcement learning 
techniques.
What does GPT stand for?
GPT stands for generative pre-trained 
transformer which is a type of large language 
model (LLM) neural network that can perform 
various natural language processing tasks such 
as answering questions, summarising text and 
even generating lines of code.
